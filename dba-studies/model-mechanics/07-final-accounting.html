7. Prices and final accounting

Realised trades are converted into prices, profits, stocks, incomes, balance sheets, GDP, and the next simulation date—in that order.

InputsProduction and realised trades from phase 6
MechanismPrices → flows → balance sheets → GDP/time
OutputsFully reconciled end-of-period model state
1. Update price indices

The output-weighted mean firm price becomes the aggregate price level; log change from the previous index is inflation. Sector indices include domestic sales and imports. Calibrated baskets produce capital and household indices.

P̄′ = Σ(PᵢYᵢ)/ΣYᵢπ = log(P̄′/P̄)P̄CF = Σg bCF,g·P̄gP̄HH = Σg bHH,g·P̄g
2. Update stocks and profits

Capital depreciates, materials are consumed, purchases replenish both, and unsold production enters inventory.

Kᵢ′=Kᵢ−(δᵢ/κᵢ)Yᵢ+IᵢMᵢ′=Mᵢ−Yᵢ/βᵢ+DMᵢΔSᵢ=Yᵢ−Qᵢ

Firm profit nets wages, inputs, depreciation, product/production taxes, and borrowing cost from sales and deposit interest. Bank profit is its interest margin; retained profit increases bank equity.

3. Set realised household income and deposits

Income is recomputed using realised wages, benefits, firm profits, and bank profit. Deposits then absorb income not spent on taxed consumption or investment, plus deposit interest and minus borrowing interest.

Dₕ′ = Dₕ + Yₕ − (1+τVAT)Cₕ − (1+τCF)Iₕ + r̄·max(0,Dₕ) − r·max(0,−Dₕ)
4. Close public, external, firm, and bank accounts

Government revenue sums social insurance, income, consumption, corporate, capital-formation, product, production, and export taxes. Benefits, consumption, and bond interest minus revenue change government debt.

L_G′ = L_G + social benefits + C_j + r_G·L_G − Y_G

Firm deposits incorporate all realised cash flows; loans amortize and add new credit; equity values deposits, materials, inventories, capital, and debt. Rest-of-world deposits record imports minus exports. The bank residual closes the system balance sheet.

5. Record GDP and advance time

The step-level aggregate GDP series receives total firm production, then the model time index increments. After step! returns, run! calls collect_data! to store the richer national-accounts measures.

Yₜ = ΣᵢYᵢt′ = t + 1collect_data!(model)
