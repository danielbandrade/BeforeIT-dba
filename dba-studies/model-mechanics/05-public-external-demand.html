5. Public and external demand

Autoregressive processes generate the government, export, and import envelopes. Calibrated product shares distribute them across buyers and sectors.

InputsPrevious demand, AR coefficients, shocks, product weights
MechanismAR update → price/inflation scaling → allocation
OutputsGovernment/export budgets and import supply
1. Government consumption

Total government consumption follows a log-AR(1) process with a fresh normal shock. It is divided equally among government entities, then scaled by the calibrated product basket and expected inflation.

C_G′ = exp(αG·log(C_G) + βG + N(0,σG))Cᵈⱼ = (C_G′/J)·Σg(cG,g·P̄g)·(1+πₑ)
2. Export demand

Total export demand uses the correlated export innovation drawn in phase 1. It is split equally among foreign consumers and scaled by the export basket.

C_E′ = exp(αE·log(C_E) + βE + εE)Cᵈₗ = (C_E′/L)·Σg(cE,g·P̄g)·(1+πₑ)
3. Import supply and prices

Real import supply follows its own log-AR process. Product coefficients split it by sector; domestic product prices inflated by expectations become import prices.

Y_I′ = exp(αI·log(Y_I) + βI + εI)Yₘ = cI,g·Y_I′Pₘ = P̄g(1+πₑ)

This phase creates desired demand and available import supply. Realised government consumption, exports, and imports are determined by matching in phase 6.

Source: government.jl and rotw.jl.
